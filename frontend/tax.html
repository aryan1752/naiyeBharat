About Tax Laws

Tax law is the set of rules that governs how individuals and businesses calculate, file, and pay taxes like Income Tax, GST, and TDS. A tax dispute includes issues like tax notices, wrong assessments, demand orders, penalties, interest, audits, GST mismatches, input tax credit denial, TDS defaults, refund delays, and scrutiny cases. It also covers appeals, hearings, and legal representation before tax authorities to resolve the matter properly.
