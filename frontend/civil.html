About Criminal Law

Criminal law deals with offenses against society such as domestic violence, assault, theft, fraud, cybercrime, dowry harassment, and other punishable acts. These cases usually involve police action, FIR registration, investigation, and court proceedings. Criminal law aims to protect victims and ensure justice through lawful punishment
